## What counts as content

- The writing: story beats, log lines, archive files, endings, guide steps and rules pages, names and descriptions
  of everything in the games, and the design documents in `docs/`.
- The art: the pixel sprites (the palette-indexed tables in the content folders) and the PWA icons made from them.
- In the tree, that is mostly `src/data/`, `src/forest-data/`, `src/late-fees-data/`, `src/fathom-data/`,
  `src/clips/content.ts`, `public/*/icons/` and `docs/`. Words written inside code files are content too.

Everything else, including the engine (`src/engine/`), all game code and the stylesheets, is code, under the MIT
license in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
